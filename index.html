Gaborone City

Gaborone is the capital and largest city of Botswana, in the country's southeast near the South African border. It is a modern, relatively relaxed city where urban life sits surprisingly close to wildlife, hills and open bushland. Botswana Tourism describes it as a growing, culturally diverse city with restaurants, nightlife, shopping, museums and live music.

Great things to do in Gaborone

  • Hike Kgale Hill - One of the city's best viewpoints. The climb takes roughly an hour and gives you panoramic views over Gaborone and the surrounding landscape; late afternoon is especially nice for the sunset.
  • Spend a day at Mokolodi Nature Reserve - About 10 km south of Gaborone, Mokolodi offers game drives, guided walks, rhino and giraffe tracking, horseback safaris and other wildlife experiences.
  • See the Three Dikgosi Monument - A major historical landmark commemorating three Botswana chiefs who travelled to Britain in 1895 to petition for protection of their territory.
  • Browse Botswana Craft - A nice place to look for locally made crafts, gifts and artwork. Botswana Tourism highlights Gaborone's craft and shopping scene as part of the city's attractions.
  • Try Gaborone's restaurant scene - The city has a surprisingly broad selection of restaurants and cafés, from local Botswana food to international cuisine. Botswana Tourism specifically highlights the variety of restaurants and nightlife.
  • Experience local music and nightlife - Gaborone has venues hosting live music by Botswana and regional artists, making evenings a good opportunity to experience contemporary Botswana culture.
